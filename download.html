Before you start

A laptop. Two phones. One local network.

The app runs on a Windows laptop. Learners connect through smartphone browsers using a shared phone hotspot, laptop hotspot, or Wi-Fi router. Internet is not required once the local network is working.

Teacher quick start

Get your first quiz running.

  1. 1
    Install and open the appRun the Windows installer and launch Classroom Quest from its desktop shortcut.
  2. 2
    Connect the three devicesTurn on a compatible phone hotspot, laptop hotspot, or router. Connect the teacher laptop and both phones to the same local network.
  3. 3
    Open the Instructions pageSelect the correct laptop address, then ask the teams to scan the join QR code. The QR code joins the quiz website, not the Wi-Fi network.
  4. 4
    Set up a matchChoose or edit the learning area, set the score target, and create your two-team battle.
  5. 5
    Play!One point is awarded per correct answer. The game tracks scores and detects the winner.

Good to know

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Two phones with a modern browser.
  • All devices must be able to reach the laptop over the local network.
  • Some hotspots block device-to-device connections; try another connection method if needed.
  • Windows may show an Unknown Publisher prompt because this community build is not code-signed.
  • Back up your saved question bank before changing installations.
Common questions

Answers before you download.

Does it require an internet connection?
No, not during gameplay. The laptop and two phones must still share a working local Wi-Fi/hotspot network. Downloads and any updates require internet access.
Do learners have to install an app on their phones?
No. They open a local website in their phone browsers. The hosting computer runs the installed Windows application.
Can I change the learning areas?
Yes. Teachers can manage subjects and edit multiple-choice questions to match their grade level and learning objectives.
Is the app really free and open source?
Yes. The current release and source code are supplied free of charge under the MIT License. The separate optional donation page does not affect download access.
Is this suitable for formal grading or research?
Classroom Quest can support practice and research activities, but outcomes must be evaluated independently. Follow school policies and relevant consent, ethics, and assessment requirements.
